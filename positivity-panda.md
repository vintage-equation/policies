# Positivity Panda — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026  
**Publisher:** Vintage Equation LLC

Positivity Panda sends you short motivational messages as notifications. It has
no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or
tracking software.

## What stays on your phone

Your focus areas, delivery schedule, mood selections, liked and disliked
messages, and delivery history are stored only on your device. Notifications are
scheduled locally by your phone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## What is sent to our server

- **Message library updates.** The app downloads its message library from our
  database, hosted by Supabase. Like any internet request, this exposes your IP
  address to the hosting provider. We do not use it to identify you.
- **Dislike feedback.** When you dislike a message and save a reason, the app
  sends us: the message you disliked, the reasons you picked, anything you typed
  in the optional text box, the app version, and a random identifier created
  when the app was installed. That identifier is not your name, email, Apple ID,
  or advertising identifier, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. Please
  do not type personal details into the text box.

We use feedback only to improve which messages the app offers.

## What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or health data. We do
not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share data with
advertisers or data brokers.

## Not a health service

Positivity Panda is not therapy or medical advice. If you are in crisis, call or
text 988 (U.S. Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or contact a qualified professional.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete
feedback you submitted, email jack@vintage-equation.com.

## Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
