# Patchwork — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026  
**Publisher:** Vintage Equation LLC

Patchwork is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts or login.

## What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, scores, wallet balances, settings, and unlocked items are
saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

## What third-party services receive

The iOS app uses three services. Each receives only what it needs:

- **Google AdMob (rewarded ads).** Ads appear only when you choose to watch one.
  We request non-personalized ads only. Google receives device and network
  information such as IP address to deliver and measure the ad.
- **Google Firebase Analytics.** Anonymous gameplay events (for example, level
  started or completed, booster used) tied to a random app-instance identifier.
  We do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA).
- **RevenueCat (in-app purchases).** Purchase history and a random app user
  identifier, used to deliver and restore what you bought. Payment itself is
  handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

## Tracking

Patchwork does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does
not ask for tracking permission.

## What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location, and we do not sell
your data.

## Web version

The browser version stores your progress in your browser only and shows no ads.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Your choices

Delete the game to remove on-device data. For questions or deletion requests,
email jack@vintage-equation.com.

## Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
